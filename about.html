About Me

Hello! I'm Derpy Hooves, the most enthusiastic mail carrier in all of Equestria. I may get things a little mixed up sometimes, but I always deliver with a smile (and sometimes a little chaos).

This site is dedicated to the noble art of package delivery, muffin recipes, and the occasional magical mishap.

Special Skills: Wall-eyed charm, wall phasing, and an uncanny ability to find packages in the most unexpected places.
